Book at cursor

Hover the chart to see the best bid and ask at that second.

Realised volatility = sqrt(sum of squared 1-second log returns × 250), annualised. At 1-second sampling the bid-ask bounce inflates it relative to 1-minute estimates, so compare instruments with each other rather than with published figures.
